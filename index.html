Chapter IV · A personal letter

For my Hayati, from my heart.

My Sarauniya,

Sometimes I think about how unexpectedly we became close. We were course mates, but we didn't really know each other. Then came a group assignment, and a small request: I asked you for your notes because I hadn't copied mine and wanted to photograph yours. It was such an ordinary thing. I couldn't have known then how much that little moment would come to mean to me.

Later, you messaged me to tell me your bag had been stolen. I tried to reassure you—to stay calm and remember that Allah knows best. Looking back, I find it beautiful that something as ordinary as notes, and a moment when you needed reassurance, became part of the beginning of our story.

I also remember how you encouraged me to study for STA121. That encouragement matters. The way you care, the way you try to help, and the person you are beyond any single moment are things I appreciate more than I may always know how to say.

I don't want to pretend that I know every page of the future. I don't. What I do know is that I am grateful our paths crossed, and I want to keep meeting what comes with patience, honesty, understanding, kindness, and faith. I hope we keep learning how to listen to each other, how to apologise when we need to, how to make room for one another, and how to choose what is good even when it takes effort.

And if Allah writes marriage for us, insha'Allah, I pray He blesses our home with sakinah, mercy, respect, laughter, and barakah. Not a life that never has difficult days, but a home where we face them with gentleness, remember Allah, and keep trying to do right by each other. I leave that future in His wisdom, and ask Him to guide us toward whatever is best.

We don't need to begin with everything figured out. We can start small—with sincere intentions, little acts of care, honest conversations, and du'a—and still build something beautiful together, one good choice at a time.

Thank you for being you, Hayati. Thank you for the encouragement you've given me and for the unexpected way you became someone dear to me. I hope this little experience reminds you that you are appreciated—not as an idea, not as a perfect person, but as the real person you are.

With love, Muhammad 💜
